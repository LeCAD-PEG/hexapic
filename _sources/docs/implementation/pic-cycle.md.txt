# The step

Positions are 2-D or 3-D, and velocities always have three components. The
two configurations are called 2D3V and 3D3V. Velocities are defined at half
steps and positions at whole steps.

## The order of a step

1. Field. The charge density left by the last push is smoothed, as set in
   [`[smoothing]`](../user-guide/case-toml.md#smoothing). The
   [field solver](../solvers/index.md) then solves the Poisson equation, and
   $\mathbf{E} = -\nabla\phi$ is computed at the nodes. The gradient uses
   central differences, and one-sided differences at a wall and next to a
   conductor.
2. Push. Each species is pushed in a single kernel. The kernel gathers the
   field at the particle, advances the velocity and the position, applies
   the walls and deposits the charge at the new position. Applying the walls
   includes absorption, reflection, emission and the charge a wall collects.
3. Exchange. Particles that left the part of the domain owned by a rank are
   sent to the neighbouring rank.
4. Sources. Beams inject particles and volumetric sources create them.
   Heating acts on the particles in its region.
5. Collisions, described in [Collisions](collisions.md).
6. Output, at the output steps.

The field solve, the particles and the collisions run on separate GPU
queues. The host waits only where it needs a result, so the stages overlap.

## Subcycling

A species with `subcycle = n` is pushed every $n$ steps with the time step
$n\Delta t$. It moves in the field averaged over those $n$ steps. Its charge
density is kept between its pushes.

## Shape

The particle shape is chosen at compile time with `HEXAPIC_SHAPE_ORDER`. The
value `1` selects CIC and `2` selects TSC, which is the default. See
[Build options](../user-guide/build-options.md). In 2-D and 3-D the weight is
the product of the weights along each axis. In 2-D a cell has the volume
$\Delta x\,\Delta y \times 1\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Loading

A `[[load]]` draws positions uniformly in its box and velocities from a
drifting Maxwellian. The Maxwellian has one temperature, or one per axis.

With `same_positions_as`, a second species is placed at the positions of the
first. The run then starts without charge separation.

## The particles in memory

The particles stay on the GPU for the whole run. They are stored as a
structure of arrays and grouped in bins of 8 × 8 cells in 2-D or 4 × 4 × 4
cells in 3-D. The position of a particle is stored relative to its bin, so
its resolution does not depend on where the particle is in the domain. The
push compacts each bin in place and moves the particles that leave it to
their new bin.

## Domain decomposition

On several ranks the grid is split into blocks, one per rank. At the seams
between blocks, the charge of both sides is summed and the halo of the
potential is exchanged. Particles that cross a seam are sent to the
neighbour after the push.
