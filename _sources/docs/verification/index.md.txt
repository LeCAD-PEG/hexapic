# Verification

HEXAPIC's results are compared with theory, with other codes and with
published benchmarks. HEXAPIC is also compared with itself: one problem must
give one answer in 2-D and in 3-D, in every orientation and on any number of
ranks.

Most comparisons belong to an {site}`example <examples/index>`. The
example's page has the setup, the commands that repeat the comparison, the
figure and the numbers. The pages of this section list the comparisons by
what the result is compared with. Each row of their tables links to the
section of the example that has the comparison.

| Page | What is compared |
|---|---|
| [Theory](theory.md) | A measured number and an analytic result. These comparisons are also the physics tests. |
| [Other codes](codes.md) | The same case, run by HEXAPIC and by XOOPIC or BIT1 |
| [Published benchmarks](benchmarks.md) | A benchmark that a paper defines, and the numbers and figures of the paper |
| [Consistency](consistency.md) | The same problem in 2-D and in 3-D, in every orientation and on any number of ranks |

Some examples have nothing to compare with. They show what the code does,
and they are listed with the others in the {site}`examples <examples/index>`.
