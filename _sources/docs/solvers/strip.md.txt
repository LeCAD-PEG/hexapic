# Strip: a direct solve

The strip solver solves the Poisson equation of a 2-D strip directly, in one
pass without iterations. An example is a scrape-off layer from divertor to
divertor. It follows the 1-D field solve of BIT1, carried over to a strip.
Its discrete operator is the one of [GMGa3](gmga3.md), so the two solvers
agree to rounding.

## The cases it solves

- Dirichlet walls at both x ends, grounded or biased
- y walls that are reflective, grounded or periodic
- a uniform permittivity
- nothing inside the domain

The ranks split the strip along x only, so every rank holds whole columns.
`type = "auto"` takes the strip solver when the case meets these conditions,
its cells split into whole bins on P × 1 ranks, and it has at most 128 cells
across y. Above that the multigrid is faster: the cost of the strip solver
per cell grows with the number of cells across.

## The method

1. Across y, the operator is diagonalised once at setup. In every step the
   charge of each column is transformed to these modes.
2. Along x, every mode $k$ is a constant tridiagonal system,

   $$
   -\phi_{i-1} + \left(2 + \mu_k \frac{\Delta x^2}{\Delta y^2}\right)\phi_i - \phi_{i+1} = \Delta x^2\, b_i ,
   $$

   with $\mu_k$ the eigenvalue of the mode and $b_i$ its right-hand side.
   Each rank solves its own columns.
3. The values at the rank boundaries follow from a small tridiagonal system
   per mode, which the ranks solve together.
4. The modes are summed back to the grid.

A step therefore needs a few kernels and one exchange between the ranks. On
one rank there is no exchange.

## Settings

| Key of `[solver]` | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `strip_exchange` | `"pcr"` | The values at the rank boundaries by parallel cyclic reduction, with messages between the ranks, or by `gather` to one rank and back |
| `strip_overlap` | `"auto"` | Exchange those values while the GPU runs the sweeps (`on`), after them (`off`), or time both on the first solve and keep the faster |
| `strip_check` | `0` | Compare the first this many solves with a multigrid solve, and print the largest difference and both residuals |
