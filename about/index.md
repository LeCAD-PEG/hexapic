# About

HEXAPIC stands for Heterogeneous Exascale Particle-In-Cell code. It is
developed at LECAD-PEG, at the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering of the
University of Ljubljana, within the HEXAPIC project. The project builds a
particle-in-cell architecture for heterogeneous HPC systems and applies it
to plasma processes with realistic geometries, collisions and transport.
