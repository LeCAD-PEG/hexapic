Scaling#

The field solver#

The 2-D field solver, GMGa3, was compared with HYPRE-PFMG, PETSc and AMReX MLMG on LUMI-G. The nodes have AMD MI250X GPUs with eight GCDs per node, and the runs use up to sixteen nodes. All solvers were measured on the same machine with the same toolchain, following a fairness protocol that is stated in the paper on the solver. The paper is in preparation.

Measurement

Result

\(16384^2\) on one node (8 GCDs)

60 ms per solve

\(16384^2\) on sixteen nodes (128 GCDs)

7.4 ms per solve

Speedup over the fastest library at the same requested tolerance

1.8 to 5.3 times

Speedup over the fastest library at the same delivered accuracy

1.6 to 3.9 times

The measurements and their job scripts are in benchmarks/scaling_study/ and paper/. These directories also contain the scripts that check the tables of the paper against the measurements.

Measuring a run#

At the end of a run HEXAPIC prints the time spent in its loop. This is the output of the two-stream case of First run on one A100:

═══ ASYNC LOOP (2000 steps, 9 store re-plans) ═══
  TOTAL [s]              min=    3.0099  avg=    3.0099  max=    3.0099
  per step [ms]          min=    1.5049  avg=    1.5049  max=    1.5049
  per step, steady [ms]  min=    1.4537  avg=    1.4537  max=    1.4537
  Throughput [steps/s]   min=  664.4753  avg=  664.4753  max=  664.4753

The minimum, average and maximum are taken over the ranks. With [debug] poisson_timing, the run also prints the share of the step that the field solve takes.

For a breakdown of the step by phase and by rank, turn on [output] performance.
